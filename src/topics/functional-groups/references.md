# References: functional groups

* Clayden, Greeves & Warren, *Organic Chemistry*, 2nd ed. (OUP): chapters on functional groups, acidity/basicity, carbonyl chemistry.
* Klein, *Organic Chemistry*, 4th ed. (Wiley): functional-group survey, IR/NMR recognition tables.
* Bruice, *Organic Chemistry*, 8th ed. (Pearson): pKa tables and spectroscopy ranges.
* Bond lengths and angles: CRC Handbook of Chemistry and Physics for lengths; ideal VSEPR angles in 3D except at centres with lone pairs, which follow one measured exemplar per class (`LONE_PAIR_ANGLES` in `src/chem/references/textbook.js`, citations in `src/chem/references/measured.js`, NIST CCCBDB): alcohols C–O–H 108.9° (methanol), ethers C–O–C 111.2° (dimethyl ether), amines 106.7° (ammonia, all three angles), thiols C–S–H 96.5° (methanethiol), acids C–O–H 106.3° (formic acid), esters C–O–C 114.8° (methyl formate). The displayed R-group molecule is usually not the exemplar; the cards quote ranges for that reason.
