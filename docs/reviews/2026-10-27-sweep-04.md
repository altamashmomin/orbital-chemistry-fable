## Scope of this sweep

Roadmap 0f, branch `core/crowded-r` ([#8](https://github.com/altamashmomin/orbital-chemistry-fable/pull/8)), the
bounded follow-up to sweep 02 finding 4. Enumerating every R-group combination showed the problem was
wider than the three molecules logged there: 1 of 100 ether, 92 of 1,000 amine and 843 of 10,000 alkene
combinations were drawn with overlapping atoms (down to 0.69 Å), silently. What changed:

* `relaxTorsions()` gains a **rescue stage** that runs only when hard contacts survive the existing
  greedy passes: for the worst pair it searches the one or two rotatable bonds nearest each atom jointly
  (30° grid, then 5° refinement) and keeps a setting only when it lowers the deepest overlap first and
  the total overlap second. Clash-free molecules never enter it. Angles are never widened; there is no
  force field.
* `stericContacts()` with `HARD_CONTACT = [1.8, 2.0, 2.5]` Å (H···H, H···X, X···X) reports what remains;
  `embedMolecule()` attaches it as `frame.crowding = { pairs, worst, groups }`. The Functional groups card
  shows a notice naming the groups, the closest contact, why the model fails and a lighter group to try,
  and says the overlap is a limit of the model, not of the molecule.
* Conformance: `<group>.crowding-flagged` (bulky-only combinations never clash silently) and
  `<group>.crowded-combinations` register notes; a combinatorial builder test (≈1,500 combinations).

`main` after #7 (`0e7863d`) was not re-swept separately: it is the squash of `7f8c1e4`, the revision sweep
03 covered. The table below is the run on the branch head.
