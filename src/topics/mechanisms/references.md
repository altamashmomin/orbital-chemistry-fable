# References: substitution and elimination mechanisms

* Clayden, Greeves & Warren, *Organic Chemistry*, 2nd ed.: chapters 15 (nucleophilic substitution) and 17 (elimination).
* Klein, *Organic Chemistry*, 4th ed.: chapters 7–8 (substitution/elimination decision table, Zaitsev vs Hofmann, solvent effects).
* Anslyn & Dougherty, *Modern Physical Organic Chemistry*: transition-state structure, Hammond postulate.
* Energy profiles in the app are illustrative shapes, not measured activation energies.
* Geometry convention: the reacting atoms of every frame are authored and pinned; the water and
  ethanol nucleophiles and bases are placed with `bentCentre()` (frameUtils.js), which reads the
  embedder's measured geometry, so they show the same H–O–H 104.5° and C–O–H 108.9° as the explorer.
  Hydroxide and ethoxide have one bond and no bond angle to compress.
